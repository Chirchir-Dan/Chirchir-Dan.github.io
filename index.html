About Me

I'm Daniel, a developer, lifelong learner, and builder at heart. My background is in Mathematics & Computer Science, and I enjoy creating software that is practical, reliable, and meaningful. Some of my projects include agricultural technology and digital tools that help preserve language and culture. Away from the keyboard, you'll find me learning languages, tending to bees, playing chess or volleyball, listening to music, or planting trees. I'm fascinated by how technology, nature, and culture can work together to improve lives, and that curiosity continues to shape both my work and personal projects.
